LEASE AGREEMENT

1. Parties. This Lease Agreement is made between the Landlord and the Tenant.

2. Rent. Tenant shall pay rent in the amount of one thousand dollars per month. Payment is due on the first day of each month.

3. Maintenance. The Landlord reserves the right to enter the Property to repair, maintain or replace any Service Media. Tenant shall keep the Property in good condition.

4. Security Deposit. Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the Security Deposit upon execution hereof.

5. Termination. The lease contract may be renewed. In this event Tenant shall request Landlord in writing within three months prior to the termination of the Contract.
